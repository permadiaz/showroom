# Phase 6D — Follow the Question

## Delivered
Ask Your Data now begins with a two-beat empty editorial stage, keeps the exact question, and follows it across the existing sources. Source evidence accumulates; three mismatch examples precede a two-beat freeze. Current-state discovery leads into connected paths, converging customer/product identifiers, period/value normalization, duplicate checks and shared business relationships. A second manual pause precedes asking the exact question again.

The answer accumulates before Show Me Why opens a coordinated evidence canvas. Existing question alternatives and investigation trails remain available. AI assistance is optional and can be skipped entirely. Technology mappings appear only after analysis, on the retained source/context field. The final reveal leads to explicit answerability discovery and at most one conditional time-to-answer probe.

## Persistent object and visual implementation
The exact question text is retained, HTML-escaped and rendered as a persistent DOM object. A second full-text question carrier moves within the same source environment. The source/world DOM subtree is reconciled in place; question movement, identifier convergence and connection changes are finite. Reduced-motion preferences disable movement. No timer advances a reveal or scene. Mobile recomposes sources and relationships vertically instead of shrinking the desktop diagram.

## Data accuracy
The approved dataset was preserved byte-for-byte. The brief's -11%, three-branch and +14% figures were illustrative alternatives, not substituted into the existing reconciled dataset. The implemented benchmark uses sales -8.4%, West's Rp 71m contribution, Category B's Rp 73.5m contribution, website visits +0.2%, and declining conversion. Region and category overlap and cannot be added. No campaign-causality, financial-loss or individual churn-risk conclusion is fabricated. All numerical evidence remains simulated business data.

## Discovery and internal guidance
Current-state customer signals use data_current_state with normalized values. Existing current_state_identified events remain supported. Final selections use data_answerability; conditional timing uses data_answer_latency. Values are validated and duplicate unchanged choices emit no extra event. Changing the primary choice clears stale timing. We can already answer it is valid and does not force an assessment. Signals are page-session-local only. Reset clears this experience's state/events without affecting others.

SELL responds to selected state, answerability and duration and remains excluded from presentation. LEARN retains the original ten modules and nine distinctions and adds AI-ready data, BigQuery, Looker, Vertex AI and Gemini. Business relevance, signals and claim limits are explicit.

## Behavior classification

Analytical and AI results are prepared simulations. Technology metadata describes possible real capabilities but no live integration is implemented. Official text labels are used, with no invented or fragile product icons.
